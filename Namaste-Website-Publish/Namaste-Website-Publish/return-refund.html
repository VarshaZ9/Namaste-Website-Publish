Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: 22 September 2026

At Namaste Nutritive Mart, we take care to ensure that your products are properly packed and delivered to you in good condition.

1. Return & Exchange

As our products are food and consumable products, we generally do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, personal preference, or incorrectly ordered products.

However, if you receive a product that is:

  • Damaged during delivery
  • Incorrect or different from the product ordered
  • Leaking or otherwise damaged
  • Expired or defective

Please contact us as soon as possible after delivery.

2. Reporting a Problem

If there is an issue with your order, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

Please provide:

  • Order ID
  • Name and contact details
  • Description of the issue
  • Clear photographs/videos of the product and packaging

Our team will review the issue and, where applicable, provide a replacement or refund.

3. Refunds

If your refund is approved, the refund amount and method will depend on the circumstances of the order and the payment method used.

For Cash on Delivery orders, we may contact you to arrange the appropriate refund method.

For online payments, approved refunds will generally be processed to the original payment method, subject to the applicable payment provider's processing time.

4. Non-Returnable Situations

Returns or refunds will generally not be available when:

  • The customer has simply changed their mind.
  • The wrong product was ordered by the customer.
  • The product has been opened, used, consumed, or damaged after delivery due to customer handling.
  • The issue is reported after the applicable reporting period, except where required by applicable law.

5. Damaged or Wrong Product

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please do not consume or dispose of it before contacting us. Keep the product and its original packaging until the issue has been reviewed.

6. Contact Us

For return, replacement, or refund-related queries, please contact us through the contact details provided on our website.

Namaste Nutritive Mart reserves the right to review each return/refund request individually and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
