Shipping Information

Last Updated: 25 September 2026

At Namaste Nutritive Mart, we aim to process and deliver your orders safely and on time.

1. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed after successful order confirmation. Processing time may vary depending on product availability and order volume.

2. Delivery Time

The expected delivery time is generally 3–7 business days from order confirmation.

Delivery time may vary depending on the delivery location, courier service, weather, holidays, or other circumstances beyond our control.

3. Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and contact information.

Please check your address carefully before placing the order.

4. Delivery Delays

In some situations, delivery may be delayed due to weather, courier-related issues, public holidays, transportation disruptions, or other unforeseen circumstances.

5. Damaged Package

If your package appears damaged at the time of delivery, please keep the product and its packaging and contact us as soon as possible.

For damaged, incorrect, leaking, or defective products, please refer to our Return & Refund Policy.

6. Shipping Charges

Any applicable shipping charges will be displayed during the ordering or checkout process before the order is placed.

7. Contact Us

For shipping-related questions or delivery issues, please contact us using the contact details provided on our website.
